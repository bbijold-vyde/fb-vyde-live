October 22, 2026 · Interview

Colin Blackwood: “Open it. Read it. Finish it.”

I am asking for the trust to be declared finished and the house to be opened. That is all. I have written to the county. I have written to anyone who will answer. Most of them do not. The house has been empty since 1974. An empty house is not a monument. It is a waste.

A locked door is a convenient way to keep a story alive. If there are papers in that room, they should have been read in 1974. If there are not, then we have been arguing about a room. I do not know which it is. I know the door has stayed shut, and I know that is useful to anyone who would rather not have the question answered.

I accept that Marcus Hale has made a claim. I do not accept that a claim is the same as a reading. He has not filed anything I have seen. He has not answered a letter. Being on the county road is not the same as being the heir.

If the trust is opened and it does not name me, then it does not name me. I can live with that. What I cannot live with is another decade of a locked door and a rumor. Open it. Read it. Finish it.

October 22, 2026 · The file

What the Blackwood fortune is

The fortune is not a number anyone has published. People familiar with the file place it in the low seven figures. Marcus Hale has not confirmed that.

It is the house: three stories of stone, a locked study, empty since November 1974. It is two parcels west of town, kept when Eleanor sold the plate works. It is the bearer bonds and the remaining cash in the Blackwood Remainder Trust. The companies are gone. There is no mill and no spur.

The trust was established in November 1974. It has never been paid out. The papers do not print an heir. They name the person she gave the key. That is what Marcus Hale has asked Vyde to keep the books on.

October 21, 2026

How Eleanor Blackwood made the money

The fortune did not start with Eleanor. Her father, Silas Blackwood, owned the spur, the scrap yard, and a small plate works. She kept the pieces when other people wanted to buy them.

After the war she sold the works and kept the land. The cash went into bearer bonds. By the early 1960s the companies were gone. What remained was the house, the bonds, and the ground.

She died in the study in November 1974. The papers do not print a name. They name the person she gave the key. That is the fortune Marcus Hale says is his. The key has still not been seen.

October 20, 2026

Marcus Hale named heir to the Blackwood fortune

Marcus Hale has the documents from the locked study. Eleanor Blackwood left them there in November 1974. They do not print his name. They say the heir is the person she gave the key.

He says she gave it to him on the porch when he was a boy, and that he used it on the study door. He has not shown the key. The family chart, drawn before Clara had a child, still lists her as having none.

He has engaged Vyde to keep the books and prepare the tax work. He has not authorized anyone else to move the funds.

Irene wants the papers read in the open. Colin wants the trust declared. Helen says the fight is no longer at the house. It is wherever those books are going to be kept.
