Death · October 26, 2026

Marcus Hale found dead
in the Vyde offices.

FB-VYDE Investigative Unit  |  Case File 26-HALE  |  October 26, 2026

Marcus Hale was found dead Friday night in the back corner of the Vyde offices on County Road. Staff discovered him after hours. The county was notified. The Register held the report until this morning.

Pages from the 1974 documents were on the desk and on the floor. There was no weapon. The office was otherwise in order. With him was the iron key to the study at Blackwood House, produced for the first time, and a note with a username and a password for his private file. FB-VYDE has taken both into evidence.

On October 2, eight point six million from the Blackwood Remainder Trust was wired into a personal account in his name. The money has not been accounted for in full. Irene Blackwood, Colin Blackwood, and Helen Voss have not given statements. No person has been named. The house is still empty. The key has now been seen.
