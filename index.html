Colin Blackwood: “Open it.
Read it. Finish it.”

FB-VYDE Investigative Unit  |  Case File 26-HALE  |  October 22, 2026

Colin Blackwood is asking for the trust to be declared finished and the house to be opened. The house has been empty since 1974. He calls an empty house a waste, and a locked door a convenient way to keep a story alive.

He accepts that Marcus Hale has made a claim. He does not accept that a claim is the same as a reading. Hale has not filed anything Colin has seen, and has not answered a letter.

If the trust is opened and it does not name him, he says he can live with that. What he cannot live with is another decade of a locked door and a rumor.
