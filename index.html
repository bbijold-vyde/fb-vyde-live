Marcus Hale named heir
to the Blackwood fortune

FB-VYDE Investigative Unit  |  Case File 26-HALE  |  October 20, 2026

Blackwood House has been empty since Eleanor Blackwood died there in November 1974. The fortune was never paid out. Marcus Hale says it is his.

He has now been inside the locked study. He brought out the 1974 documents. They do not print his name. They say the heir is the person Eleanor gave the key. He has not shown the key.

He has engaged Vyde to keep the books and prepare the tax work. Given the size of the fortune, FB-VYDE has been asked to investigate whether he really is the heir.
