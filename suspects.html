Persons

Named so far. Office players who signed up are not listed until they are given a statement.

Marcus Hale

Found dead Friday night in the Vyde offices.

He claimed the Blackwood fortune. On October 2, eight point six million from the Blackwood Remainder Trust was wired into a personal account in his name. The iron key to the study was with him. It is the first time the key has been produced. A note with a username and a password for his private file was with him. Both are in evidence. The money has not been accounted for in full.

Irene Blackwood

Family. Written onto the chart later.

The land should have come to her side in 1974. She has never been inside the study. She does not accept a later claim. She wants the papers read in the open. She has been told of the death. She has not given a statement.

Colin Blackwood

Family. Written onto the chart later.

He wants the house opened and the trust declared finished. A locked door is a convenient way to keep a story alive. He did not ask for Marcus to be the one holding the key. He has been told of the death. He has not given a statement.

Helen Voss

Married into the family.

She will not say if she tried the study door. She wants the question answered by someone who can keep a file. If Marcus has the documents, the fight is wherever the books are going to be kept. She has been told of the death. She has not given a statement.

Eleanor Blackwood

Died in the house, November 1974.

She placed the fortune in the Blackwood Remainder Trust. She did not print a name. She named the person she gave the key.
